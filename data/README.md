# Data provenance

This folder holds processed inputs used directly by the simulation. The raw source
files live in `raw_data/` (not redistributable; ask the project lead) and the
intermediate calibration targets live in `calibration_data/`.

## Sources

| Source | Provider | Years | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| UNAIDS country estimates | UNAIDS AIDSinfo / Spectrum estimates | 1990–most recent | National HIV prevalence, incidence, n_infected, new infections, deaths, PLHIV by age. |
| PHIA surveys (SDHS 2007, SHIMS 2011, PHIA 2016, PHIA 2021) | Eswatini Ministry of Health / ICAP at Columbia | 2007, 2011, 2016, 2021 | Age/sex-specific HIV prevalence and incidence with 95% CIs. 2021 is held out for validation. |
| Population counts | Eswatini Central Statistical Office / UN World Population Prospects | 2000–2015 (census), extended via WPP | Population by age, sex, year — used for demographics and scaling. |
| National ART coverage | Eswatini National AIDS Program / PEPFAR | 1990–2024 | Number of people on ART by year (`n_art.csv`); drives the `ART` intervention. |

## File-by-file

| File | Provenance | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `eswatini_hiv_calib.csv` | Generated by `python utils.py` from `raw_data/`. | Combined calibration targets used by `run_calibrations.py`. |
| `n_art.csv` | National ART numbers (1990–2024). | Used by the ART intervention. |
| `art_coverage.csv` | Stratified ART coverage by age, sex, year. | Direct input to `sti.ART`. |
| `condom_use.csv` | Condom-use probability by partnership type and year. | Input to the sexual network. |
| `init_prev_hiv.csv` | Initial HIV prevalence by risk group, sex, and SW status. | Seeds the epidemic at sim start. |
| `eswatini_age_1985.csv` | Age distribution at sim start. | Used to initialize the agent population. |
| `eswatini_asfr.csv` | Age-specific fertility rates. | Drives pregnancies/births. |
| `eswatini_deaths.csv` | Background mortality rates by age/sex/year. | Non-HIV deaths. |
| `eswatini_migration.csv` | Net migration by age/sex/year. | Demographics module. |

## Licensing

All sources are publicly released aggregate epidemiological estimates with no
individual-level identifiers. UNAIDS data is released under the [UNAIDS Open
Data Policy](https://www.unaids.org/en/dataanalysis/datatools); PHIA microdata is
governed by the [PHIA Data Use Agreement](https://phia-data.icap.columbia.edu/).
Aggregate statistics derived from PHIA (the form used here) are released for
public use. Census/WPP and ART coverage are public.
